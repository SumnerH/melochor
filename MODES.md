# Modes

Melochor's default presets select the following visualizer modes. Several modes
have more than one preset; those presets configure a different aesthetic or
reactivity profile while using the same underlying mode.

## Pond

A calm, music-reactive evening pond. It combines water ripples, rain, fish,
birds, foliage, and atmospheric lighting. The default **Pond** preset uses a
tranquil palette and disables rockets.

## Space Invaders

A retro arcade scene with marching alien formations, a defender, shields,
motherships, projectiles, and beat-synchronized routines. Audio energy drives
the scene's movement, lighting, and animation.

## Fire Plasma

A procedural flame scene with selectable flame algorithms and music-driven
height, color, and spark behavior.

- **Fire Plasma** uses a realistic fire presentation with rockets enabled.

## Fireworks

The classic fireworks visualizer: launched shells, explosions, trails, and
scripted firework routines.

- **Fireworks** is the standard realistic fireworks preset.
- **Glory** is a neon, gravity-free variation with long trails and diamond
  stars.

## Tunnel Wormhole

A forward-moving cosmic wormhole with tempo-responsive travel, colored wall
gems, sparks, and deep-space rarities. Its routines include lightning, gem
spark explosions, pastel rainbow walls, and an exterior aurora effect.

- **Wormhole** is the default tunnel preset.

## Mandala Sacred

A kaleidoscopic particle mandala with rotational symmetry and sacred-geometry
inspired routines.

- **Mandala** uses twelve slices and a realistic palette.
- **Trance** uses four slices, tranquil colors, long trails, larger diamond
  stars, and stronger particle reactivity.

## Underwater Lava

An underwater environment with bubbles, algae, jellyfish, and marine rarities.
It responds to the analyzed audio spectrum and stereo field.

- **Underwater** is the default underwater preset.

## Synaesthesia Classic

A starfield-based visualization where musical activity produces colored,
animated stars and waves.

- **Synaesthesia** uses small five-point stars and wave fading.

## Random

The **Random** preset is a selector rather than a visual mode. It periodically
chooses from the available presets and can also change presets around eligible
track climaxes.
